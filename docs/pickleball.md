# My Pickleball

**My Pickleball** (Personal Growth in the sidebar, or **More** on a phone) tracks
your own pickleball: your DUPR, where you are on each part of the game, repeatable
benchmark tests with targets, and your matches and partners. It is private to your
account and is kept out of the Students list.

The first time you open it, enter your current DUPR doubles and singles ratings
(either can be left blank) and press **Start tracking**.

## DUPR

Copy your rating from the DUPR app whenever it changes (**Add rating**). Ratings
can have up to three decimals (4.123). Each kind (doubles, singles) shows the
latest rating, the change since your first entry and a chart. Open **All doubles
ratings** to edit or delete an entry; a deleted rating can be brought back with
**Undo**.

## Focus next

The card works out your next level from your latest doubles DUPR (4.5, then 5.0)
and compares your latest benchmark results with that level's targets. The biggest
gaps come first. Tests you haven't measured yet are listed, and each opens the
form to record a result.

The targets are a starting point drawn from coaching sources, not an official
standard. Your "how points were lost" tallies and KAP are worth checking alongside
them.

## Benchmarks and targets

| Test | 4.5 target | 5.0 target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crosscourt dink rally without a miss | 30 balls | 50 balls |
| Third-shot drops into the kitchen | 7/10 | 8/10 |
| Resets from mid-court against drives | 6/10 | 8/10 |
| Deep returns | 8/10 | 9/10 |
| Deep serves | 7/10 | 8/10 |

You can also add your own tests. Repeat the same test every few weeks to see the
trend.

## Matches

Each match records doubles or singles, your partner, whether it was DUPR-rated,
the opponents, the score, and notes and insights. The page shows:

- your record overall and for doubles and singles;
- your **record with each partner**, matched ignoring capitals and extra spaces,
  with your latest note about playing with them;
- **How you lose points**: optional rough counts per match for serve or return
  errors, third-shot errors, resets popped up or missed, dink errors, speed-ups
  too early, lost hand battles, lobs or overheads, and other. The page totals
  your last 10 matches, biggest first.

### Kitchen arrival (KAP)

This is optional. For each of your third shots, count whether you and your
partner both got to the kitchen line balanced (or won the point first). Count
"arrived" or "didn't arrive" separately for drops and drives. If you didn't split
them, put everything in one row. You can also count third-, fifth- and
seventh-shot errors.

**KAP = third shots where you arrived ÷ all third shots.** The page shows your
KAP across all matches, drops compared with drives, and a KAP trend over time.

Your pickleball profile, ratings, matches and results are included in **Download a backup** and **Restore from a backup file**.
