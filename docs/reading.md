# Reading

**Reading** (Personal Growth in the sidebar, or **More** on a phone) keeps track of
the books you're reading and the ones you've finished. It is private to your
account.

## Yearly goal

Press **Set a goal** and enter how many books you want to finish this year. The
goal card shows how many you've finished, a progress bar, how many are left, and
whether you're ahead of or behind a steady pace. For example, 24 books a year
means about 2 a month. Press **Change** to edit the goal or remove it (you can
Undo a removal). Each year has its own goal.

## Adding books

**Add book** opens the form:

- **Title** (required) and **author**.
- **Status:** **Finished** or **Reading now**.
- **Genres:** tick as many as fit, for example WLW and Mystery, or WLW and Romance.
  The list has common genres. Type your own (e.g. WLW) and press **Add genre**;
  it's offered for every book after that.
- **Dates:** when you started (optional) and, for a finished book, when you
  finished.
- **Rating:** 1–5 stars, or no rating.
- **Note** (optional).

## Currently reading

Books you're reading appear in **Currently reading**. When you finish one, press
**Finished it**, pick the date and, if you like, a rating. It then moves to your
finished books and counts towards your goal.

## What the page shows

Pick a **year** at the top to see:

- books finished that year and all time;
- your top genre and your average rating;
- a chart of books finished each month;
- **What you read**: your genres, most read first. A book with two genres counts
  once for each, so a WLW mystery adds to both WLW and Mystery;
- the list of books finished that year, newest first. Search it by title or
  author, or filter it by genre. Use **⋯** to edit or delete a book (with Undo).

Your books and goals are included in **Download a backup** and **Restore from a
backup file**.
