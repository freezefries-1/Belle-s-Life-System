# Install belle's life system on your phone or computer

The app can sit on your Home Screen (or in your Dock) and open in its own
window, without the browser's address bar. It's the same app and the same
account — nothing to download from an app store, and updates arrive
automatically.

Address: **https://belle-life-system.vercel.app**

## iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open **Safari** (it must be Safari — other browsers on iPhone can't add web apps).
2. Go to **https://belle-life-system.vercel.app** and sign in.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up — at the
   bottom of the screen on iPhone, top right on iPad).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Check the name says **Belle's Life**, make sure **Open as Web App** is on
   (newer iOS versions show this switch), then tap **Add**.

The blue three-steps icon appears on your Home Screen. Opening it shows the
app full-screen with the bottom navigation bar above the home indicator.

You may need to sign in once more inside the installed app — iPhone keeps
its sign-in separate from Safari's.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome** and go to **https://belle-life-system.vercel.app**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Tap **Install**.

## Mac, Windows (Chrome or Edge)

Open the address and click the install icon at the right of the address bar
(a monitor with a down arrow), or choose **⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install
page as app**. On a Mac with Safari 17 or later: **File → Add to Dock**.

## What works offline

Being installed doesn't make the app work without a connection. Your data
lives in your account, so the app needs the internet to load it. If you open
it with no connection you'll see a "You're offline" screen with a
**Try again** button. Changes you'd already made are kept on the device and
saved once you're back online (the sync indicator in the top bar shows this).

## Removing it

Press and hold the icon, then **Remove App** (iPhone) or **Uninstall**
(Android). Your data is untouched — it's in your account.
