# Students (tennis coaching)

The **Students** page (sidebar on a computer, **More** on a phone) tracks each
student's progress on four tracks, with your lesson notes. It's private to
your account, like everything else in the app.

## The tracks

Each student moves through the tracks at their own pace, so an adult who
rallies well but can't serve yet shows progress on Rally even while Serve
stays at S1–S2.

**Rally** (the main progression)

| Stage | Working on | Ready to move on when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 Contact | Fed balls, forehand and backhand: grip, ready position, swing shape | 7 out of 10 fed balls over the net, both sides |
| R2 Short court | Service line to service line, centre to centre | 10-ball rally |
| R3 Reading the bounce | Short court, both sides, small steps to the ball | 20-ball rally; moves their feet, doesn't reach |
| R4 Move back | Baseline, centre to centre | 10-ball rally |
| R5 Baseline steady | Baseline, centre to centre | 20-ball rally; half the balls land past the service line |
| R6 Small movement | One or two steps left and right, back to the middle | 10-ball rally with movement; split step and recovery |
| R7 Direction | Crosscourt forehand and backhand, then down the line | 10+ crosscourt each side; changes direction when called |
| R8 Depth and spin | Topspin, depth, side to side | 7 out of 10 deep; rally 15+ moving side to side |
| R9 Live ball | Rallies under pace; attacking short balls, defending wide balls | Keeps rallies going under pressure; picks the right ball to attack |

**Serve:** S1 Toss and motion · S2 From the service line · S3 From the
baseline · S4 Reliable · S5 Placement · S6 Spin second serve · S7 Under
pressure.

**Net:** N1 Ready at the net · N2 Volley to volley · N3 Volleys off
groundstrokes · N4 Approach and overhead · N5 Net in points.

**Match play:** M1 Scoring · M2 Points from a rally · M3 Games with serve ·
M4 Sets and tiebreaks · M5 Club competitions · M6 Tournaments.

**Overall level** (shown on each student's card) comes from the Rally stage:
Beginner R1–R3 · High beginner R4–R5 · Intermediate R6–R7 · Advanced R8 ·
Competitive R9 plus competition play (Match play M5 or above).

## Using it

- **Add student:** a first name or nickname is enough. Choose Junior or
  Adult and the ball (red, orange, green or yellow); hand, backhand, start
  date, goals and notes are optional.
- **On a student's page:**
  - each track shows the current stage, since when, what they're working on
    and what "ready to move on" looks like;
  - **Moved up to R6** records the next stage today (with Undo);
  - **Choose stage…** records any stage with a date and an optional note, for
    example when you first assess a new student.
- **Lesson note:** what you worked on, what went well, what to work on next.
  Quick to add on your phone straight after a lesson.
- **Progress history** lists every stage reached, newest first. If you
  recorded something by mistake, **Remove entry** (with Undo); the current
  stage steps back to the previous one.
- **Students who stop lessons:** set their status to **Paused** or
  **Finished** (Edit details) to keep their history. **Delete** removes the
  student, their history and lesson notes (with Undo straight afterwards).

Students and lesson notes are included in **Download a backup** and **Restore
from a backup file**.

## Privacy

This is information about other people, some of them children. Keep it to
what helps your coaching: first names or nicknames, no medical details or
parents' contact details. Your backup file includes it, so keep that file
private too.

## Not yet

Stage names and "ready" tests are fixed in this version (Stage 1). Editing
the ladder in the app, plus matches, benchmarks and charts, are planned as
later stages.
