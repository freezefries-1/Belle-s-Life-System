# Recovery & Sleep

Each day, copy two numbers from your WHOOP app: **recovery %** and **sleep %**.
The app keeps them, shows today's numbers on the **Today** page, and shows your
trends on the **Recovery & Sleep** page (Personal Growth in the sidebar, or
**More** on a phone). The numbers are private to your account.

There is no automatic connection to WHOOP. The numbers are typed in, which takes
a few seconds a day.

## Adding a day

- On **Today**, press **Add today's WHOOP**. On the Recovery & Sleep page, press
  **Add today** (or **Add a day** once today is in).
- Enter recovery % and sleep % as whole numbers from 0 to 100. You can leave one
  of them blank.
- To fill in or fix an earlier day, change the **Date**. If that day already has
  numbers, they appear so you can edit them. There's one entry per day.

## Colours

Recovery uses WHOOP's colours:

| Colour | Recovery  |
| ------ | --------- |
| Green  | 67–100%   |
| Yellow | 34–66%    |
| Red    | 0–33%     |

## What the page shows

- Average recovery and sleep for the last 7 days and the last 30 days. Days you
  didn't enter are skipped, not counted as 0.
- Day-by-day bars for the last 30 or 90 days, with recovery bars in their
  colour. A gap means nothing was entered that day.
- **Recovery colours**: how many green, yellow and red days you had in that
  period.
- **All days**: every entry, newest first. Use **⋯** to edit or delete a day
  (with Undo).

Your days are included in **Download a backup** and **Restore from a backup file**.
